How it works

From camera roll to closet. Automatically.

No photographing hangers. No manual tagging. Velour recognizes the clothes you already wear in everyday photos.

Velour builds your wardrobe
Scanning libraryOn-device · 4,212 of 12,480
Step 1

Scan

Velour reads your photo library, on your device and offline where possible, looking only for clothing.

Wool coat · CamelClassic · Tailored · from 37 photos
Step 2

Recognize

AI identifies each item, merges duplicates seen across hundreds of photos, and learns its color, fabric and style.

Step 3

Curate

Your wardrobe appears, organized and searchable. Then Velour starts styling looks from what you already own.
